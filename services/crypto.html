Cryptocurrencies are digital tokens created through software protocols, enabling secure transactions that are difficult to counterfeit or reuse. This form of trading emerged only recently, with Bitcoin, released in 2009 at less than a cent, being a prominent example. Despite awaiting a significant surge, cryptocurrency trading has gained widespread popularity.

Cryptocurrency trading offers significant earning potential. For instance, Bitcoin reached a market cap of $160 billion by November 2017. The advantage lies in the relatively low number of traders, preventing market saturation.
