James Richarlson

Innovative Chief Executive Transforming the Globe!

Visionary CEO who is revolutionizing the world!

With more than 35 years immersed in the banking industry, James now harnesses his deep expertise and passion for cryptography. His goal? To unite the world while providing opportunities for all to create a dependable secondary income through his enthusiasm for cryptocurrency. James emerged from challenging circumstances, yet his fierce ambition to secure financial freedom for his family blossomed in his childhood. Recognizing James unwavering determination, his elder brother stepped in, offering financial support through work on construction sites. This sacrificial support enabled James to channel his energies into education, intensifying his hunger for success and deepening his dedication to elevating his family's prospects.

After earning his degree, James entered the banking industry as an asset management executive. It was during this time that he crossed paths with his colleague, Jack Arsten. Since the start, their rapport was strong, blossoming into a great friendship. Yet, James found himself unsatisfied with his role at the bank. Disappointment settled in as he recognized that the bank prioritized profits over the well-being and advancement of its customers. The banking system burdened clients with numerous inconveniences: dwindling interest rates amidst annual inflations, a lack of privacy, and exclusionary practices that troubled James deeply.

The outlined frustration fuelled an idea that shall soon change the world! James idea, the push behind Premuim Asset Managers, is giving everyone the means to generate enough passive income to subsist on. The idea, however, has thrust James on a collision course with the banks; one that demanded he gets assistance before proceeding. He asked his old friend Arsten to help him found an equalized financial system, the opposing force that offers the solutions to fight the banks elfish regimes. Arsten quickly recognized the potential of the idea and its many benefits and agreed to join James. Both retired from the bank towards the end of 2014 and started working on Premuim Asset Managers. Their vision is to change the world. A vision to offer people equal earning possibilities. To provide an opportunity for people to simply earn money without much effort. Their vision sees everybody a family like James included, having the opportunity to afford the luxury. This is the vision that drives Premuim Asset Managers!

Jack Arsten

CFO & Co-Founder of CryptoConnect

The Master of Financial Management!

Unlike JAMES RICHARLSON, Jack Arsten grew up in affluence in America. He is the son of a onetime leading investment banker in the US, and he showed signs of following after his father right from the onset. Arsten started delving into the business world from a young age. As an adolescent, he made the firm decision to emulate his father's path, aspiring to step into his father's shoes once the older man retired!. Arsten's fervor for numbers is unmistakable, adeptly navigating their complexities. Educated in a private institution, he was generously equipped by his father with ample financial support, nurturing aspirations to follow in his role model's footsteps. Commencing his journey at 18, he pursued Economics, specializing in Banking and Finance. Fueled by an unwavering ambition, he embarked on internships both locally and internationally, showcasing his drive from a tender age.

After graduating, Arsten delved into the banking industry, serving as an asset management executive. He intentionally chose a different bank than his father's, aspiring to ascend to his father's role and perhaps surpass it independently, without leaning on familial connections. His strategy proved fruitful! In just six years, Arsten rose to a significant position of authority. He held this role for an additional two years, during which his achievements and expertise captured his father's notice.

In no time, he ascended to the role of executive director at the bank where his father was employed. During his tenure, he crossed paths with his colleague, James Richarlson. Arsten found it hard to envision departing from his current position, given the immense effort he had invested. Nevertheless, the concept of Premium Asset Managers deeply intrigued him. Eventually, both he and James departed from the company to embark on establishing an entirely novel form of financial service entity. Their vision was to create an inclusive institution, welcoming everyone to participate. They held firm beliefs that the substantial volatility within the cryptocurrency market presented the ideal conditions for them to manifest the services they envisioned. Arsten, the CFO of crypto vest trades, takes pride in his decision to leave the traditional banking system, seeing it pay off with over $7.3 million in investments and a user base exceeding 210,070. The venture's growth statistics continue to climb, a testament to his strategic choices.
